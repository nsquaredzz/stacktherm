What the total resistance rests on

Each class of input is raised by 10 % and the stack re-solved. Elasticity is the percent change in total resistance per percent change of that input; the effect multiplies it by an assumed uncertainty for the class. Read it as a ranking of which inputs to pin down first.

Conductivity of amorphous SiO₂ computed from atoms, with no fitted transport parameter. A 648-atom glass is quenched by molecular dynamics; its vibrational modes, the velocity operator between them and their three-phonon linewidths go into the Wigner expression, in which heat is carried by wave-like tunnelling between modes rather than by a phonon gas. The same force constants are also run through kALDo, an established open-source package, as the reference backend.
